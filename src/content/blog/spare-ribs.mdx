## Equipment & Timing Overview

Using parchment paper on your baking sheet prevents sticking and traps the natural juices as the ribs roast. Flipping midway ensures an even, golden crust on both sides.

| Item | Method | Heat | Total Time | Key Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hamilton Spare Ribs** | Oven (Parchment & Tray) | 425°F (220°C) | **20–25 mins** | Flip at the 12-minute mark for even cooking |

> [!NOTE]
> Lining your baking tray with parchment paper before placing the ribs keeps the marinade or dry rub from burning onto the sheet pan.

---

## Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

1. **Prep the Tray & Preheat:** Oven set to 425°F (220°C).
Preheat your oven. Line a heavy baking tray with a sheet of parchment paper. Arrange the prepared Hamilton family spare ribs in a single layer on the tray, leaving small gaps between the pieces for even air circulation.


2. **First Roast Phase:** 12 minutes.
Place the baking tray on the center rack of your oven. Bake undisturbed for **12 minutes** until the tops begin to brown and render their juices.


3. **Flip & Finish:** 8 to 13 minutes remaining.
Carefully pull out the tray and flip every rib over. Return to the oven and roast for an additional **8 to 13 minutes** (reaching 20 to 25 minutes total) until the meat is fully cooked, tender, and slightly caramelized at the edges.

## Pro Tips for Quality Control

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If applying a sugary sauce or glaze, brush it on during the final 5 minutes of baking to prevent the parchment paper or glaze from scorching.

* **Parchment Protection:** Always use parchment paper over aluminum foil when baking seasoned ribs to avoid acidic sauces reacting with the metal.
* **Doneness Check:** The ribs are ready when the meat has slightly pulled back from the bone tips and an instant-read thermometer reaches an internal temperature of at least **63°C to 71°C**.
