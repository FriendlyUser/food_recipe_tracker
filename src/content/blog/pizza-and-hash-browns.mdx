## Equipment & Timing Overview

Using the microwave pre-cook step thaws and heats the pizza interior rapidly, allowing the oven stage at **400°F** to focus entirely on melting the cheese and crisping the crust without drying it out.

| Item | Appliance / Method | Temperature | Total Time | Key Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Pizza** | Microwave + Oven | `400°F` (`204°C`) | **~17 mins** (incl. precook) | Microwave first, bake to finish |
| **Hash Brown Patties** | Air Fryer | `400°F` (`204°C`) | **12 mins** | Flip halfway (6-minute mark) |

> [!NOTE]
> Combining a brief microwave thaw/precook with a **17-minute total process time** in a 400°F oven ensures the pizza crust gets properly baked and golden while keeping the prep time short.

---

## Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

### 1. Personal Pizza (Microwave + Oven Method)

* **Step 1 (Precook):** Place the frozen pizza in the microwave on high for **1–2 minutes** to thaw the core and soften the dough.
* **Step 2 (Bake):** Transfer to a preheated oven at `400°F` (`204°C`).
* **Step 3 (Finish):** Bake until the total elapsed time reaches around **17 minutes** (including the precook phase), or until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is fully melted and bubbling.

### 2. Frozen Hash Brown Patties (Air Fryer Method)

* **Mode:** Air Fry
* **Temperature:** `400°F` (`204°C`)
* **Time:** `12 minutes` total
* **Action:** Lay patties in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Flip at the **6-minute mark** for even crispiness on both sides.

---

## Pro Tips for Timing Both Together

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Start your oven preheating first. Put the hash browns into the air fryer right around the time the pizza goes into the oven so both finish hot at the exact same time.

* **Single-Layer Air Frying:** Do not overlap hash brown patties in the air fryer basket; keeping space between them ensures maximum convection air flow and extra crunch.
* **Oven Rack Height:** Place your personal pizza on the center rack of the oven to get an even balance of bottom crust crisping and top cheese browning.
