When preparing eggs where a fully set yolk is required, using controlled timing on the stovetop ensures the yolk cooks completely through to a solid center without becoming rubbery or dry.


## Equipment & Timing Overview

Cooking eggs to a completely firm yolk requires precise heat control. Hard boiling uses a post-boil steep method to prevent green ring formation, while the over-well method utilizes low heat and a covered pan to set the yolk top-to-bottom.

| Item | Method | Temperature / Heat | Total Time | Key Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hard-Boiled Eggs** | Stovetop Pot | High to Boil, then Off | **12 mins** (steeping) | Cover pan and remove from heat immediately at boil |
| **Over-Well Eggs** | Stovetop Skillet | Medium-Low Heat | **5–6 mins** | Flip halfway and gently press yolk to test firmness |

> [!NOTE]
> Moving hard-boiled eggs directly into an ice bath right after the 12-minute steep halts the residual cooking process instantly and makes peeling effortless.

---

## Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

### 1. Hard-Boiled Eggs (Fully Set Yolk)

* **Step 1 (Boil):** Place eggs in a single layer in a saucepan and cover with cold water by 1 inch. Bring water to a rolling boil over high heat.
* **Step 2 (Steep):** As soon as the water boils, cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and remove it completely from the heat source. Let it sit undisturbed for **12 minutes**.
* **Step 3 (Cool & Peel):** Transfer eggs to a bowl filled with ice water for **5 minutes**. Gently tap the shells to break and peel under cool running water.

### 2. Over-Well Fried Eggs (Fully Set Yolk)

* **Mode:** Stovetop Skillet
* **Heat level:** Medium-Low
* **Time:** `5–6 minutes` total
* **Action:** Melt oil or butter in a non-stick skillet. Crack eggs into the pan and cook for **3 minutes** until whites are mostly opaque. Carefully flip, gently puncture the yolk centers with a spatula, and cook for an additional **2–3 minutes** until the yolk is entirely firm.

## Pro Tips for Quality Control

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Ensure all cookware, utensils, and oil or butter used are naturally free from cross-contamination to keep the dish entirely safe and gluten-free.

* **Puncture test for over-well:** Puncturing the yolk center slightly after flipping allows direct contact with heat, guaranteeing no liquid yolk remains inside.
* **Ice bath timing:** Do not skip the ice bath for hard-boiled eggs; rapid cooling contracts the egg white inside the shell, stopping the cooking process immediately so the yolks stay bright yellow rather than greyish-green.
